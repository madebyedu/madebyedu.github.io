OutletCars Business Card Design

OutletCars was a newly launched brand selling used cars online. As they began meeting clients in person, they had no physical material to leave a lasting impression or reinforce their brand.

I shaped every visual decision on the business card design, translating the existing brand guidelines into a print-ready format that felt consistent with their digital presence.

The final card gave the brand a professional, credible look in face-to-face interactions, helping the business feel established even in its early days.
