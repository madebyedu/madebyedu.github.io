From photography to graphic design

I'm Eduardo Carrasco, a graphic designer based in Barcelona. Studying photography taught me how the placement of elements within an image shapes the way we see it. It's something I still consider whenever I'm designing.

Background & approach

I've spent much of my career working in the automotive industry, designing for different brands across a range of formats. A social media graphic and a printed catalogue have very different requirements. Much of my work has involved adapting brand guidelines to suit those needs.

I work primarily with Adobe's creative tools for graphic design, video editing and motion graphics. I'm also continuing to develop my HTML and CSS skills through the creation of landing pages and email newsletters.

I'm open to opportunities in other industries and willing to relocate.
