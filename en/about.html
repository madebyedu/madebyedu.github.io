Background & approach

Since 2018, I’ve worked on visual communications in the automotive industry, adapting brand guidelines to different formats and audiences. My work ranges from editorial layouts and campaign assets to video editing and motion graphics.

I use Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign for design and image-making, and Premiere Pro and After Effects for video and motion. I’m also developing my HTML and CSS skills through landing pages and email newsletters.

I’m open to relocation and new opportunities across different industries. Get in touch.
