Centro Porsche Valladolid corporate brochure

Client
Centro Porsche Valladolid
Role
Editorial design and application of Porsche’s brand guidelines.
Deliverable
A printed catalogue for customers visiting the dealership for the first time.

Context

When customers first walked into the new dealership, the team wanted to welcome them with something in print that told the story of the centre and showed what services were available. The opening in Valladolid also brought Porsche into the Volkswagen Group Retail Spain network for the first time.

Design decisions

The catalogue groups information by topic, and the typography sets headings apart from the body text. On the services page, bullet points make it easy to find a particular service at a glance. A vertical photograph beside the list shows the inside of the dealership while giving the text enough space to read comfortably.

The front cover shows the dealership’s façade so readers can recognise the building at a glance. On the back cover, a photo of the interior sits alongside the contact details.

Outcome

The catalogue was printed and ready to hand out at the opening. Customers could take a copy home after their first visit and refer to it later for the dealership’s services and contact details.
