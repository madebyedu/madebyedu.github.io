Water-repellent glass treatment campaign

Client
Volkswagen Group Retail Spain
Role
Visual direction and graphic design for Volkswagen, Audi and SEAT.
Deliverable
Facebook and Instagram posts, stories, web banners, rear-view mirror hangers and a folded leaflet for the dealership.

Context

When customers in Leioa brought their cars in for a service, Volkswagen Group Retail Spain wanted to offer them a rain-repellent treatment as an optional extra. The pilot covered Volkswagen, Audi and SEAT. The campaign was designed to introduce the treatment online and explain what it involved at the dealership.

Design decisions

I designed the campaign materials using the supplied copy and chose colours and photographs to suit each brand. I then adapted the layout to fit square posts, vertical stories and horizontal banners.

At the dealership, a short message on the rear-view mirror hanger introduced the treatment inside the car. The leaflet gave customers more detail about the treatment and the options available.

Outcome

Each brand had its own version of the campaign for the Leioa pilot. Customers could find out about the treatment online and look through the options at the dealership.
